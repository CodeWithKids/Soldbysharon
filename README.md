About Us

SoldBySharon is a premium real estate and property advisory brand based in Kenya, dedicated to serving diaspora clients seeking secure, transparent, and high-value property investments back home.

We understand the unique challenges of investing in property from abroad—distance, trust, and reliable execution. That’s why we offer a seamless, end-to-end experience that goes beyond buying and selling. From sourcing verified opportunities to managing and protecting your investment, we act as your eyes, ears, and trusted partner on the ground.

Our focus is simple: to help you invest confidently, build wealth, and stay connected to home—without the stress.
